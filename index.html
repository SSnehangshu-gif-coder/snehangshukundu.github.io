Reproducibility is the foundation

Most of my work at Uppsala University centred on making experimental outcomes consistent and traceable — not just getting a result once. I treat every protocol as a living document that reflects how things actually run, not how they ran the first time.

GMP-aware mindset

Formal GMP training through Svensk Medicin AB, combined with hands-on experience in SOP writing, deviation handling, and structured documentation. I understand why a regulated lab works the way it does and I adapt to those requirements without needing a long onboarding.

NGS from sample to data

Teaching and knowledge transfer

HOW I WORK

Working together

Freelance engagements are most effective when there is clarity upfront. Here is how I typically approach a new project. 

01

Brief call

We talk through the scope, the timeline, and what success looks like for you. 30 minutes is usually enough to figure out whether it makes sense.

02

Scoped proposal

I send a written summary of what I will deliver, when, and at what rate. No vague estimates — clear deliverables and clear terms.

03

Execution

I work structured and communicate proactively. If something changes or a problem comes up, I flag it early — not after the deadline.

04

Handover

Clean documentation, readable outputs, and a proper handover. Everything is traceable and ready to use or build on.
